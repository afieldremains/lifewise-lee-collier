# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Local supporters and prospective donors in Lee and Collier counties, Florida, considering support for LifeWise Academy.

## Product Purpose
Explain LifeWise Academy's Bible-based character education during public-school hours and invite local support for its work in Lee and Collier counties.

## Positioning
LifeWise partners with parents to offer Bible classes during the school day through released-time religious instruction.

## Operating Context
Visitors arrive on a campaign landing page and can continue to LifeWise Academy's official local-program giving checkout.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- This project is a static campaign page; donations are processed by LifeWise Academy.
- Whether a LifeWise program is currently operating in either county has not been verified. Do not imply that a local program or chapter is active.
- The official giving checkout may require donors to select an eligible local program. Direct visitors to LifeWise for help confirming program availability or gift designation.

## Brand Commitments
- Use the LifeWise Academy name and the red, yellow, and blue brand palette.
- Use the user-supplied LifeWise classroom and bus photos as campaign imagery.

## Evidence on Hand
- LifeWise Academy's official website describes Bible-based character education during the school day and names courage, kindness, and respect as character traits.
- User-supplied LifeWise classroom and bus photos are stored in `assets/`.
- User-supplied community volunteer photo is illustrative and must not be represented as a local LifeWise event.

## Product Principles
- Make the during-school-hours program clear before asking for a gift.
- Keep local county context visible without inventing local operating facts.
- Send donors to LifeWise Academy's official giving flow.
- Make the page readable and usable on mobile and with a keyboard.
